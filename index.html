How to read the court

Blue is your team. The number tells you which player. Red is the defense. X1 guards player 1. Run (a cut) Dribble Pass Screen: stand still like a wall

For grown-ups: use the step buttons to go one move at a time and ask “Who’s open now?” before pressing next. On a keyboard, press / to search, Space to play or pause, and the arrow keys to step.
