📖 Words to know

How to read the court

Blue is your team. The number tells you which player. Red is the defense. X1 guards player 1. Run (a cut) Dribble Pass Handoff: hand the ball to a teammate Screen or box out: stand still like a wall

For grown-ups: many plays stop and ask a question. “Who’s open?” comes before a big pass: kids tap the open player on the court (or press 1–5). “Where should 2 go?” comes before a big move: kids tap the spot on the court where that player should run. Switch off “Questions” to just watch. Tap a player, or use Follow, to watch just that player’s job. “Read aloud” reads each step out loud for kids who are still learning to read. The 🖨️ button prints the play, step by step, for practice. Under each play, “Words to know” explains its basketball words, and “See all the words” lists every one. Use the step buttons to go one move at a time. On a keyboard, press / to search, Space to play or pause, and the arrow keys to step. Want to draw your own play? Try the play editor. Once this site has loaded, it works without internet too, so you can bring it to the gym.
